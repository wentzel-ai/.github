## Companies

`Wentzel.ai` is a brand. Products are built and operated under two companies:

- **Wentzel Investments LLC** (Florida) — operates Cytra, Lawvora, Humanome, MAPS Hub, CARL, Q by Wentzel, Vector, and CashMeUp.
- **Centaris Health Inc.** (Delaware) — **operates OculiRX exclusively.** Centaris is structured as a separate company so all medical-device regulatory, billing, and contractual matters for OculiRX sit inside a dedicated entity.
